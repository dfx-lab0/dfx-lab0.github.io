Feixiang Du is faculty at [School of Electrical and Information Engineering](https://www.tlu.edu.cn/), [Tongling University](https://www.tlu.edu.cn/). Feixiang Du obtained the M.S. degree from [Taiyuan University of Technology](https://www.tyut.edu.cn/) in 2018. He is pursuing a Ph.D. at [Swinburne University of Technology](https://www.swinburne.edu.au/) in 2023. Currently, Feixiang Du is leading the [Deep Fusion eXploration Laboratory](https://DFX-Lab.github.io/) and exploring how to understand and interact with the world via the computer vision. His research interests include Object Detection, Image Segmentation and Hyperspectral Reconstruction.
